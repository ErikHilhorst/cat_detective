# Playtest round 1 (2026-11-01) - simulator, blind LLM testers

Build under test: new framing (cold open + news-van roll call), gathering finale (6 steps),
Rudebeak-in-the-cases ending. Testers: Haiku (`2026-11-01_haiku_playtest.md`) and Sonnet
(`2026-11-01_sonnet_playtest.md`), both blind, simulator-only. Both reached the ending.

## What worked
- The Big Lie arc lands (Sonnet): bedroom/entrance plant "alive at 8", the living-room
  speaker breaks it, Reyes's "suspiciously unanimous" seeds doubt.
- Best beats named by testers: Basil's false confession ("case closed"), Marsh's
  "the cases are EMPTY" blurt.
- Gathering boards: 0 wrong submits for both testers - readable, never stuck.

## Problems (merged, by impact)
1. **Culprit too obvious too early (Sonnet).** Basil's "television people wheeling big black
   boxes" + garden cases + the pool schedule naming "D. Marsh - Library" turn the library into
   a victory lap. -> Room contracts: decide where Marsh may be *suspected* vs *named*.
2. **Gathering boards too easy / pre-answered (Sonnet, 0 wrongs).** "A full half hour before
   his own call" states the inference; nothing ties Marsh to 6:30 in a source. -> Gathering
   sentences need the same "pose the tension, never the answer" rule as room boards; add
   decoy pressure; tie 6:30 to a source the player reads.
3. **6:30 / 7:00 arithmetic felt like a guess (Haiku)** - the 7:00 call lives only in the
   pool area (often last). Matches the audit WARN: `time_630pm` not guaranteed found.
4. **Ambiguous room slots:** entrance slot 5 (Crew Arrivals vs 6:30 PM), living room
   "gone before [six]" (6:00 / 6:30 / Missed Snack / Settled Feathers), bedroom slot 5
   (7:20 vs Manifest Entry), library HOW bank has one (wrong) chip until Marsh's topic.
5. **Boards restate source text (Sonnet): entrance and kitchen.** Same finding as the
   structure readout (R2/R5).
6. **Loose threads:** documentary camera, bright feather, the squeaky-wheels spritz, shot list,
   Petra's "dark and padded" callback (her topic is clue-gated, may be unheard).
7. **Sound Guy reads as absent from the roll call (Haiku)** - he is only a face in the lineup.
   Consider one neutral crew line naming "the sound guy" without a hook.
8. Post-solve confrontations are easy to miss in text (the game has toasts + speech bubbles;
   the simulator now names the room on every board message).
