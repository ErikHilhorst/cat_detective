## 1. Opening

Stakes are clear: a locked cage, a sunrise deadline, and "somebody in there is lying to me." The news-van roll call introduces all ten suspects with one hook each, and the three case questions (who / how out of a locked cage / where is he now) map well onto the play. Reyes's "a poultry matter" and the cat-slips-through-the-gate beat set the tone nicely. I did not notice the Sound Guy in the lineup as a suspect, which is the intent. The intro is long (about 20 beats of banners) but reads quickly.

## 7. Top 5 problems with concrete fixes

1. **The boards are too easy, and the wording gives the answers away.** All 7 room boards and all 5 gathering boards were solved first try, and in several sentences the answer is the only chip that matches the sentence's phrasing ("Only one sound has ever beaten his headphones" -> squawk). Fix: rewrite the sentences so the clue they point to is one of two plausible chips, e.g. in the Bedroom board, drop "Only one sound has ever beaten his [headphones]" and state the contradiction instead.
2. **Library gate has no visible reason on the door.** "Locked for now" plus a Wexler shout from a different room is the only hint. Fix: change the Library door's LockedText to name the pool area or Wexler ("Wexler: the crew is still out by the pool, kitty"), so the gate reads as a reason, not a wall.
3. **The Sound Guy's topic menu skips option 2.** The menu shows 0, 1, 3 when the gated option is hidden, which reads as a bug. Fix: renumber the visible options contiguously in the menu renderer, or keep the gated option's number but show it as a dimmed entry (similar to visited topics).
4. **The Hidden Speaker is discoverable in one inspect and then the squawk is obviously fake.** The bedroom board ends on "the squawk at eight" as the alibi, but the living room hands over the speaker immediately. Fix: make the speaker's inspect text less direct (drop "taped to the couch frame where only somebody at whisker height would ever look") so the player has to connect the cable to the audio desk, which is the design intent.
5. **Gathering order differs from CLAUDE.md.** The first gathering step is the squawk-at-8 board, not Chip. If intended, update the design doc; if not, the script may be out of sync with the sim's expectation. Fix: verify `case_script.json` gathering step order and the sim's `gather` flow match the intended Poirot order.
