## 7. Top 5 problems with concrete fixes

1. **Chip has no visible gathering board.** His beat plays inside the intro, but the Chip step (per the design) seems not to appear as a board. Fix: confirm the Chip board is present in the gathering order, or move the contract/commission content into a real board step.
2. **Library gating is unexplained.** The door was locked, then open, with no toast or hint. Fix: add a `LockedText` on the living room -> library transfer that says what to do, or a toast when it opens.
3. **Several board slots have near-identical answers** (Coco's Sighting vs 6:45 PM; Settled Feathers vs Missed Snack; Foam-Lined Cases in Garden as a WHAT answer). Fix: make the first word-bank clue of each slot's category more distinct, or add one more decoy per category in the Living Room and Pool Area.
4. **Marsh is not hinted in the library until Wexler's topic.** The pool call sheet lists "D. Marsh - library" but the connection to the Sound Guy badge is only made in the library. Fix: the crew call sheet could mention "audio setup" so the player can connect the Sound Guy in the library.
5. **Finale gives no wrong-answer feedback in testing.** Because I had no wrong submits, I cannot confirm the feedback text. Fix: test one deliberate wrong gather in the audit and confirm the "N/M fit" line appears in gather steps.
