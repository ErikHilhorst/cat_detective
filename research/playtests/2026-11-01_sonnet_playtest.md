# Playtest report - Sonnet - "The Missing Macaw" (text sim) - 2026-11-01

Played start to finish, ending reached. Room order: Entrance, Bedroom, Living Room, Garden, Kitchen, Pool Area, Library, then the Gathering. Total wrong submits: 2.

## 1. Opening
- Stakes were clear: "Find out who took him, how he got out of a locked cage, and where he is now. Before sunrise." Three questions, a deadline, a sealed house. Strong.
- The news-van roll call worked. Each cast member got one hook ("carries a key to every door", "famously opinionated about cages", "more carts and road cases than a moving company"). Later I recognised everyone instantly.
- Slightly too pointed in hindsight: "whatever is hidden in that house could roll out with them" plus the "road cases" line telegraphs the cases/crew answer. It did not feel like a spoiler at the time but it primed me.
- The intro states "last heard squawking at eight o'clock sharp" as fact. That plants the big lie cleanly, but it also means every later 8:00 mention feels pre-flagged.
- Nothing under-explained. D. Marsh is not named in the roll call (only "sound"), which is fair.

## 2. Room by room

### Entrance (0 wrong, difficulty 1)
- Question as understood: "Is the bird still inside, and what does the house paperwork say?" Answer: sealed since 8:15, nothing left through the gate.
- Micro: all four sources carried a fact (radio = gate + 8:15, registry = household + 6:30, Reyes, Chip). Reyes's three topics were the best content here.
- Meso: the board restated what I had just read ("whoever stole X is Still Inside these walls", "Reyes says somebody's night in that book Does Not Add Up"). Vocabulary matching, not deduction. Only the last slot (PR Disaster vs Chip's Commission) was a mild choice.
- Macro: moved the case (bird is inside; registry smells off). Reyes's confrontation "Suspiciously Unanimous" ("every soul in this house heard the same bird at the same minute... Honest witnesses never agree that neatly") is a superb early seed.
- Loose: Chip's "Which is exactly why I would never LOSE him" reads as an alibi; resolved in the Gathering.
- Confusing: Reyes's "Somebody's evening in this book does not add up". I read it as sloppy data, because the crew column is "hurried, overlapping, hard to count".
- Suspects after: nobody. Chip motive negative (commission).

- Dangling: Documentary Camera; Bright Feather; Shot List; Abandoned Spritz (squeaky wheels); Petra's finale line "Dark and padded, I said. Quiet as a stone." (implies she advised on keeping a bird quiet; never explained, she has no confrontation); how Marsh knew the bird was loose (he "found him at the bird bath" is implied only).

## 8. Top 7 problems
1. **Culprit obvious by room 4-6.** Hidden Cases + Basil's "television people" + Coco + schedule name Marsh before the Library. Fix: gate the tarp/cases behind the Library solve (or Marsh's topic), cut "television people" from Basil's first confession, and keep "D. Marsh" off the Pool schedule (use "Sound dept - Library").
2. **Gathering boards pre-solve the deductions.** "A full half hour before his own call" and "rolled past the bedrooms" state the inference. Fix: ask "He was inside by ___. His call was at ___." and let chips carry the subtraction.
3. **Bedroom slot 5 ambiguous** (7:20 vs Manifest Entry). Fix: reword to "the ___ entry in the manifest" or make 7:20 a distinct role.
4. **Library: only-wrong-option bank.** Audio Cable was the lone HOW/WHAT until Marsh's topic. Fix: put Unplugged Headphones on the Mixing Board / Marsh's intro or add a decoy so the topic is not required.
5. **Entrance and Kitchen boards are restatements.** Fix: pose a question that needs two sources (Kitchen: whose wheels at 7:00, needing grease).
6. **Dead / unpaid clues:** Documentary Camera, Bright Feather, Abandoned Spritz squeak, Shot List, Spent Earplugs, Petra's "Dark and padded". Fix: pay or cut; give Petra a gated topic.
7. **Text and sim glitches:** Rosa "that night"; "Coco's Sighting" chip before meeting Coco; `topic "The Sound Guy"` rejected; Marsh <-> 6:30 never linked in a source (add a registry line for the sound department).
