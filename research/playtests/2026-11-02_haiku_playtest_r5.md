## 7. Top 5 problems with concrete fixes

1. **Boards are too easy; the sentence echoes the answer chip.** Every room was solved first try, with 0 wrong submits across all seven boards. Fix: keep the sentences but add one plausible decoy per slot category in each word bank (e.g., in the garden add a second WHERE/WHEN chip like "Settled Feathers" to the bank as a near-miss for slot 2; in the pool area give the WHO slot a second candidate).
2. **Dead chips in the pool-area bank.** "Straw Hat in Frame" (WHO) and "Bright Feather" (HOW/WHAT) are in the bank but no slot uses those categories. Fix: either add a slot that uses them (e.g., a WHO slot asking whose hat it was) or move them out of that room's bank.
3. **The "EMPTY" crew tag undercuts Marsh's slip.** The garden case tag says "EMPTY - RETURN TO TRUCK", so "the cases are EMPTY" is confirmed by a clue, not caught in a lie. Fix: change the tag to a neutral label ("STUDIO 4 - FRAGILE - RETURN TO TRUCK") so the library slip is the only place the empty claim appears.
4. **Gathering step count/order looks off.** I saw five boards. The first board ("The Squawk at Eight") came straight after the Chip beat ("bird-shaped signature", "Manager's clean"), and the Chip step had no board of its own. Fix: check whether the Chip step's board is missing from the sim/game flow, or whether it is merged into the intro. If intended, say so in the script.
5. **Topic menu numbering skips.** In the library, D. Marsh showed options 0, 1, 3 (2 was hidden behind a gate), and Petra showed 0, 1 with 2 and 3 hidden. Typing a hidden number returns "That option is not available" with no hint. Fix: renumber visible options sequentially, or show locked options greyed out with a short hint ("needs the garden board").
