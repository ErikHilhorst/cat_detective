# Playtest R2 (Sonnet) - "The Missing Macaw"

Result: finished the case, ending reached. Zero wrong submits on any of the 7 room boards and all 6 gathering steps.

Honest caveat: the zero is not a sign of a perfect design. It mostly means the boards are very guessable. Nearly every board is solvable by reading the sentence frame and matching category tags (WHO/WHAT/WHEN) against a bank of 2-4 chips per category. I never needed to weigh a theory to submit.

## 1. Opening
- The 8:00 PM squawk -> 8:15 empty cage -> news-van roll call is strong. Stakes are clear: "whatever is hidden in that house could roll out with them" at sunrise. Reyes's brief gives three questions: who took him, how he got out of a locked cage, where he is now. Those three questions anchor the whole game.
- The roll call helped later, but as a name list. Every suspect gets one line of tease (Rosa "carries a key to every door", Basil "famously opinionated about cages", Derek "not a bird person"). I used these as a checklist of suspects, not as a source of clues.
- Over-explained: "more carts and road cases than a moving company" plus "whatever is hidden in that house could roll out with them" hands the player "cases/carts = the vehicle" in the first minute. I was watching the cases from then on. That is good for stakes but it narrows the mystery.
- Under-explained: nothing told me I could (or should) return to a room after solving. Confrontation topics appear silently, and Chip's bubble stayed lit after I had apparently exhausted him (see problem 6).
- Not mentioned in the roll call: the film crew sound/lights staff as individuals. D. Marsh only appears as "The Sound Guy" in the Library, so he did not feel like part of the opening cast.

## 2. Room by room (visit order: Entrance, Bedroom, Entrance, Living Room, Library, Kitchen, Garden, Pool Area, Library)

- Difficulty 2/5. Wrong submits: 0. I resisted "Crew Arrivals", "North Gate Locked" and "Household Sign-In" as decoys, but only because the slot tags made that easy.

- Belief: during the Bedroom, because the board sentence ends in "right?" and I was writing it into the slots.

- Note: the lie is dismantled in room 3 of 7, so it is not a "big lie" that carries the whole game. The bigger lie, "Basil/Rosa did it", is solved in the finale.

## 8. Top 7 problems, ranked by impact

1. **The culprit is on the table by the Library (room 4 of 7) and the Garden hands over the hiding place (room 6).** The 3-hour mystery is a 1.5-hour mystery plus a 1.5-hour confirmation. Fix: keep the scribble ambiguous across the early rooms (done) but move the Library's Sound Guy evidence to the end. Make the library a neutral "Wexler and the crew" room whose board does not name Marsh. Make the badge reveal come from the Pool Area. Also change the Garden "Hidden Cases" text so the cases are not obviously the container: leave out "Silence. The deep, upholstered kind." until the confrontation topic, and let the Garden board be about the shed/boot prints only.
2. **Boards are solvable by category grammar, not by deduction.** 0 wrong submits across 13 boards. Slots are tagged by category and each category has only 2-4 chips, often only 1-2 decoys. Fix: add real near-misses per category (two plausible times, two plausible signatures) so that at least one board per room requires reading a clue's text. The 6:45/6:47 and 7:00/7:20 pairs are good models; add them to the Bedroom and the Entrance.
3. **The finale re-asks boards the player has already answered.** Steps 1 and 2 are the Entrance and Living Room boards reworded. Fix: cut step 1, and make step 2 require a new synthesis (e.g. the Living Room speaker + the Library cable + Derek's headphones) so it feels like the speaker deduction being applied to a suspect, not repeated.
4. **A confrontation clue is granted without the scene ("The Cases Are EMPTY" at the Library solve), and its related topic references a place I had not visited.** This breaks the "the player hears the slip" moment. Fix: gate the clue strictly on the topic; and gate the topic on having visited the Garden or having Foam-Lined Cases. The tarp-and-shed wording only works after the Garden.
5. **Loose threads clutter the notebook:** 5:45 Herb Run, Crew Arrivals, North Gate Locked, Documentary Camera, Treat Jar bribery, Lamp Returns, Wexler's Ratings, Bright Feather. They pad the word banks and never get closed. Fix: either tie each to a deduction (5:45 herb run initialed B. -> Basil came in from the garden just before 6, which becomes the latch time; 7:20 lamps -> a third wheeled thing that Derek counts) or cut them to a one-line flavour description that gives no clue chip.
6. **Bubble and topic discoverability.** Chip's speech bubble stayed on with no apparent content; his third topic appeared only after the Bedroom solve. Petra's "third" topic was not available. The gated topics show up with no signal that I should return. Fix: make a short toast when a solve unlocks a topic (the sim prints "your deduction corners X", which helps) and only show a bubble when a topic is really available. Add a bubble hint on first entry after a solve.
7. **Gathering feedback is a bit mechanical, and two WHO slots in the final board are interchangeable scribbles.** I filled "Initials on the Log", "Early Signature" and "Scrawled Signature" in whatever order and nothing told me which was right. Fix: give each of the three scribble clues a distinct, checkable attribute (a unique detail such as ink color or a corner flourish on the log) so a wrong pairing fails, or merge them into two clues.
